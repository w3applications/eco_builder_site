How it plays

Where you build is the puzzle.

Every level is a small board and a budget of blocks. What you build matters. Where you put it matters just as much.

  1. Homes need power and water. A home only fills with people when the city has both to spare. Without them it stays empty.
  2. Neighbours change everything. A park makes the homes beside it bigger. A factory beside a home does the opposite.
  3. Blocks are your budget. Each extra copy of the same building costs more. Factories earn you more blocks, at a price in smog.
  4. The board fights back. Rivers double a pump. Rock gets in the way. Every board is a different trade-off.

The pieces

New buildings arrive a few at a time, so no level asks you to learn everything at once.

  • Home

    Houses 100. Needs 1 power and 1 water, or it stays empty.

  • Park

    +40 people in each home beside it. Filters smog from the polluters next door.

  • Solar

    +5 power. Clean, and it never runs out.

  • Pump

    +4 water, or +8 beside a river. Uses 2 power.

  • Factory

    40 extra blocks to build with. The catch: it makes smog, and each home beside it holds 40 fewer people.

  • Recycling

    20 extra blocks, and it removes 2 smog. Uses 2 power.

  • Transit hub

    +40 people in each home beside it, and several hubs add up. Uses 1 power.

  • Arcology

    Houses 500. Needs 6 power and 5 water. Ignores its neighbours.

  • River

    Cannot be built on, but a pump beside it gives double.

  • Rock

    Cannot be built on. Plan around it.

  • Toxic ground

    Makes smog unless a park is on it or beside it.

  • Coal plant

    Already there and cannot be removed. Plenty of power, and smog to deal with.

No manual

See exactly why.

The rules are taught by the city itself. You never have to guess what a number means or why it moved.

  • A line of advice, always. It says what your city needs next: more water, a park, or fewer homes beside the factory.
  • Tap the score. See how it adds up and what is holding it back. Press and hold any tile for its own numbers.
  • Three stars to earn. Every level was solved before it shipped, so the targets are set against the best known city.
  • Three hints per level, never more. The stars you earn are yours.
  • Any screen, either way up. Portrait or landscape, on phones, tablets and foldables.

Is VerdaWorld really free?

Yes. The three chapters (36 levels) and the daily puzzle are free, and there are no ads. The level packs, the Green Pass and themes are optional one-time purchases.

Do I need to be online?

No. Every level works offline, including the daily puzzle. You need a connection only to compare your daily score with other players, to buy something, or to restore purchases.

Is the Green Pass a subscription?

No. It is a single purchase that you keep. Nothing in VerdaWorld renews or bills you again.

Can I pay for a better score?

No. Purchases add levels, hints and looks. They never change the rules or the tools you have on a level, and hints are capped at three per level, or one on a daily puzzle, however many you own.

How do I move my purchases to a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple or Google account, open Settings in the game and choose Restore purchases. Level progress is stored on the device, so it stays with the device.
