Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information VerdaWorld handles when you play, why, and who processes it. It covers the game on iPhone, iPad and Android, and this website.

The short version

  • There is no account. The game never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • There are no ads, the game does not use an advertising ID, and we do not sell information about you.
  • Your levels, stars and settings are saved on your device.
  • Daily puzzle scores are stored against a random ID, not a name, and are set to be deleted after about 35 days.
  • The game sends usage statistics and crash reports so we can fix and improve it, and purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

1. Who we are

VerdaWorld (the "game") is published by W3 Applications Inc. ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at games@w3applications.com.

2. What stays on your device

Your game progress is saved on your device and is not sent to us. This includes the levels you have played, your stars, your boards in progress, your Eco Point and hint balances, your badges, your daily streak and your settings.

Your device's own backup service, such as iCloud or Google's device backup, may include this data if you have backups turned on. That is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

3. What the game sends

When your device is online, the game sends the following. None of it includes your name, email address or contacts.

WhatDetailsWhy
Daily puzzle scores When you finish a daily puzzle: the date and difficulty of the puzzle, your score, your stars, how many hints you used, and the time it was submitted. It is stored against a random ID created for your copy of the game. To tell you what share of players you beat, and to allow one score per player.
Usage statistics Events such as starting or finishing a level, using a hint, opening the shop or the daily puzzle, and making a purchase. They come with general device information: device model, operating system version, game version, language, and country or region. They are linked to an app-specific ID, not to an advertising ID. To understand which levels are too hard or too easy, find problems, and decide what to build next.
Crash reports If the game crashes: what the game was doing at the time, the device model, operating system version, game version, and an ID for the installation. To find and fix bugs.
Purchases If you buy something or restore purchases: which product, when, the price and currency, the store country, and the store's transaction identifiers, linked to a random ID. Your payment is taken by Apple or Google. We never receive your card details. To deliver what you bought and to restore it on another device.

As with any internet service, the companies that run these services receive your device's IP address when the game connects to them.

4. What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Your precise location
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone or camera
  • An advertising ID. The game has no ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Notifications and sharing

Reminders. If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, such as a note that the day's puzzle is ready. They are created and stored on the device itself. We do not send them from a server, and you can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

Sharing a result. When you share a daily result, the game hands a short piece of text to your device's share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice, and is covered by the privacy policy of the app you share it with.

6. Who processes it

We use a small number of companies to run the game. They process the information described above on our behalf, under their own terms and privacy policies.

CompanyWhat it does for the game
Google (Firebase) Stores daily puzzle scores (Cloud Firestore), creates the random ID they are stored against (Firebase Authentication), and provides usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). Privacy and security in Firebase
RevenueCat Checks purchases with the store and keeps track of what each installation owns. RevenueCat privacy policy
Apple App Store Takes payment and delivers purchases on iPhone and iPad. Apple privacy policy
Google Play Takes payment and delivers purchases on Android. Google privacy policy

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a sale or merger of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply to it.

These companies may process information in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection rules from the country where you live.

7. Keeping and deleting

  • Game progress stays on your device until you delete the game or clear its data.
  • Daily puzzle scores are set to be deleted automatically about 35 days after they are submitted.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are kept only as long as we need them to improve the game, within the retention periods offered by Firebase.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to restore your purchases, and as tax and accounting rules require.

To ask us to delete information linked to your copy of the game, write to games@w3applications.com. Because the game has no account, we may need your help to work out which records are yours.

8. Children

VerdaWorld is suitable for a general audience and is not aimed specifically at children. The game does not ask anyone, of any age, for a name, email address or other contact details, and it shows no ads.

Purchases need the approval of the store account holder. We recommend that parents use the purchase controls built into their child's device.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can play every level, including the daily puzzle, with your device offline. Your score is only compared with other players when the game can reach the internet.
  • You can turn notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • You can delete the game at any time, which removes the data stored on your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address below. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Earlier versions

Versions of VerdaWorld before 3.0 had a public leaderboard. If you took part, your display name and scores were stored for it. Version 3.0 no longer uses the leaderboard. To have an old leaderboard entry removed, contact us.

11. This website

verdaworld.app does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and does not load anything from other companies' servers. The playable level on the home page runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details when you visit, and keeps logs for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Links to the App Store and Google Play take you to Apple's and Google's own sites, which have their own policies.

12. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to tell you, such as a notice in the game or on this website.
